# thevalheim.wiki

Find your way through Valheim, plan your next trip and get more from the materials you gather.

## Keep your bearings while you play

The Realtime Map from seed companion mod puts your character and nearby creatures on your world map. Keep it open on a second screen as you explore.

![The connected Valheim map showing the player, nearby creatures, their stars and distances](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/valheim-wiki/live-seed-map/main/listing/screenshots/map-player.png)

[Open the map](https://thevalheim.wiki/map) · [Set up Realtime Map from seed](https://thevalheim.wiki/guides/live-seed-map) · [Download the mod](https://github.com/valheim-wiki/live-seed-map/releases/latest)

## Find the next place to explore

Looking for Burial Chambers? Search from where you stand and see the nearest places first.

![Burial Chambers ordered by distance from the player](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/valheim-wiki/live-seed-map/main/listing/screenshots/search-results.png)

[Search your world](https://thevalheim.wiki/map)

## Know where your portals lead

Select a portal pair to see both ends and the connection between them.

![The selected Moder portal pair linked across the map](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/valheim-wiki/live-seed-map/main/listing/screenshots/portals-moder.png)

## Plan before you gather and craft

Try your gear and three foods in the planner to see your health, stamina and armour before you spend your materials.

![A bronze set, Iron Mace and three foods in the loadout planner](https://thevalheim.wiki/assets/guides/loadout-and-food-planner/bronze-set.png)

[Plan your loadout](https://thevalheim.wiki/planner)

Add the pieces you want to build and get one list of the materials to gather.

![A forge corner and its combined material list](https://thevalheim.wiki/assets/guides/build-cost-calculator/forge-corner.png)

[Count your building materials](https://thevalheim.wiki/build-cost)

## Check your next catch

Choose the fish, your fishing skill and your stamina to see what it takes to land it.

![Northern Salmon stamina calculation with the fishing skill and hat selected](https://thevalheim.wiki/assets/guides/fishing/salmon-fight.png)

[Plan a fishing trip](https://thevalheim.wiki/fishing)
